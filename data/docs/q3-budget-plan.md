# Orbit Labs: Q3 Budget Plan

This plan sets the spending limits for the third quarter. It is confidential to Finance and the CEO.

## Department budgets
Engineering: INR 1,20,00,000. Sales and Marketing: INR 65,00,000. HR: INR 18,00,000. Legal: INR 22,00,000.

## Special project
Project Marigold, the new analytics product, has a budget of INR 2,34,75,000 for the quarter. Spending above this amount needs written approval from the CEO.

## Spending rules
Any single purchase above INR 5,00,000 needs approval from the Finance Head. The quarterly forecast is reviewed on the 5th working day after the quarter ends.
