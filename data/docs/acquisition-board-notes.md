# Orbit Labs: Board Notes on a Proposed Acquisition

These notes are restricted to the Executive team and Legal Counsel. Do not discuss them outside this group.

## Proposal
The board is considering acquiring Skylark Analytics, a Bengaluru startup that builds dashboard software. The proposed price is INR 74,60,00,000, paid half in cash and half in shares.

## Reasons
Skylark Analytics has 140 customers and would let Orbit Labs launch its analytics product a year earlier than planned.

## Legal review
Legal Counsel must complete due diligence before the board vote. The review covers contracts, intellectual property and pending disputes.

## Timeline
The board vote is planned for the first week of December. No announcement may be made before the vote.
