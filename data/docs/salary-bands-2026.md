# Orbit Labs: Salary Bands 2026

This document lists the pay for each band. It is confidential to HR, the Finance Head and the CEO.

## Bands
Band L1 (Intern): stipend of INR 25,000 per month.
Band L3 (Engineer): base pay of INR 9,20,000 per year.
Band L4 (Manager): base pay of INR 13,10,000 per year.
Band L5 (Senior Manager): base pay of INR 18,47,350 per year.

## Review cycle
Salaries are reviewed once a year in April. The standard increment budget for 2026 is 8 percent of total payroll.

## Exceptions
Any pay above the top of a band needs written approval from the CEO.
