# Orbit Labs: Restructuring Plan (Project Willow)

This plan is restricted to the Executive team and the HR Manager. It must not be shared with employees before the announcement date.

## Summary
Project Willow will remove 38 roles across Sales, Marketing and Operations to cut costs by 12 percent. Engineering is not affected.

## Severance
The total severance budget for Project Willow is INR 4,82,60,000. Each affected employee receives 3 months of pay plus 1 week for every year of service.

## Timeline
Affected employees will be told on 14 January. The HR Manager prepares the individual letters by 7 January.
