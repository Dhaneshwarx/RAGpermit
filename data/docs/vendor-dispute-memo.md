# Orbit Labs: Vendor Dispute Memo (Privileged)

This memo is restricted to Legal and the CEO.

## Background
CloudNest Services, a hosting vendor, claims Orbit Labs owes INR 58,20,000 for unpaid invoices. Orbit Labs disputes INR 22,00,000 of this amount because of repeated service outages.

## Case
The matter is registered as Case LX-5519 before the commercial arbitration panel in Chennai.

## Settlement position
Legal recommends settling. The maximum settlement Orbit Labs is willing to pay is INR 41,75,000. This figure must not be shared with the vendor.

## Next steps
Legal will send a without-prejudice offer to the vendor's counsel within 10 working days.
