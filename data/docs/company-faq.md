# Orbit Labs: Company FAQ and Holidays

Orbit Labs Pvt Ltd is a fictional software company with offices in Chennai and Pune.

## Office hours
The Chennai office is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Reception closes at 5:30 PM. The Pune office follows the same hours.

## Holidays in 2026
Orbit Labs observes these holidays: 26 January (Republic Day), 12 March (Foundation Day), 15 August (Independence Day), 2 October (Gandhi Jayanti) and 25 December (Christmas).

## Visitors
All visitors must register at reception and wear a visitor badge. Visitors must be accompanied by an employee at all times.

Email hello@orbitlabs.example for general questions about the company.
