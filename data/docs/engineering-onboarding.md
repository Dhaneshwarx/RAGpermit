# Orbit Labs: Engineering Onboarding Guide

This guide is for new members of the Engineering department.

## First week
On day one you receive a laptop and access to the code repository and the team chat. Your assigned buddy will walk you through the development setup. Complete the security training by the end of your first week.

## Code review rules
Every change needs at least one approval from a teammate before it is merged. Changes to the payment module need two approvals.

## Daily stand-up
The Engineering team holds a 15-minute stand-up every weekday at 10:00 AM in the chat channel #eng-standup.

## Who to ask
Ask your buddy first. For access problems, contact the IT helpdesk at it-help@orbitlabs.example.
