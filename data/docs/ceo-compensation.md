# Orbit Labs: CEO Compensation

This document is restricted to the Executive department. No other role has access.

## Package for 2026
The CEO's total package for 2026 is INR 3,91,25,000. This is made up of base pay, an annual bonus and stock options.

## Bonus rules
The annual bonus is up to 40 percent of base pay. It depends on revenue growth and on customer satisfaction scores. The board compensation committee approves the bonus each April.

## Stock options
The CEO holds options on 2 percent of company shares. The options vest over 4 years.
