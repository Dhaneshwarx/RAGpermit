# Orbit Labs: Leave Policy

This policy applies to all employees of Orbit Labs Pvt Ltd.

## Casual leave
Employees get 12 casual leave days per year. Casual leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 paid sick leave days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## Earned leave
Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave per month. Up to 30 earned leave days can be carried over to the next year.

## Applying for leave
Apply for leave in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.
