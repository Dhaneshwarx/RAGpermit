## Assigned role

Fictional example: Arjun is the Junior Engineer and reports to Deepak. Arjun works on development tasks, fixes defects and writes automated tests. Deepak reviews Arjun's changes before they are merged.

## Development workflow

Arjun starts from an assigned issue with acceptance criteria, creates a development branch, implements the change and runs the relevant tests. Each review request includes the issue reference, a summary of the change and test results.

## Access boundaries

Junior engineers use the development environment and approved sample data. Production runbooks require Engineering clearance 2 and are not available to the Junior Engineer at clearance 1. Interns at clearance 0 cannot read this guide.

## Weekly goals

Arjun discusses goals with Deepak each Monday at 10:30 AM. The weekly update is due Friday at 4 PM and covers completed tasks, test coverage, review feedback and blockers. A blocker lasting more than two working hours is escalated to Deepak.
