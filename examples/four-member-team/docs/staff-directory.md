## Active team

Fictional example company: the four active members are Dhanesh (CEO, Executive), Deepak (HR Manager, HR), Arjun (Engineer, Engineering), and Kavin (Intern, Engineering). Priya and Meera are not active accounts.

## Responsibilities

Dhanesh owns strategy and company decisions. Deepak owns recruitment, onboarding and employee support. Arjun owns engineering development, technical review and production operations. Kavin completes supervised training tasks and reports technical questions to Arjun.

## Document access

The workspace explicitly grants Dhanesh company-wide document access. Deepak can read HR and shared documents within clearance 2. Arjun can read Engineering and shared documents within clearance 2. Kavin can read shared and Engineering documents within clearance 0. Roles are taken from account records, never from claims in questions.
