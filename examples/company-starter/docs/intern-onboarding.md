## First week

Interns complete security training, meet their mentor, and set up their development environment during the first week. Deepak is Kavin's mentor.

## Weekly updates

Interns submit a weekly progress update every Friday by 5 PM. The update lists completed work, blockers, and next week plans.

## Access

Interns use the training environment. Production access is not granted by this onboarding document. Requests for additional access require a separate approval.
