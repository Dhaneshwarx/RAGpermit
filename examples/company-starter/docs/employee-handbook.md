## Leave

Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted to the reporting manager at least two working days in advance, except emergencies.

## Working hours

Normal working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Flexible hours require approval from the reporting manager.

## Support

Employees contact HR for leave and onboarding questions. Technical access requests go to the Engineering lead. Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the Engineering lead.
