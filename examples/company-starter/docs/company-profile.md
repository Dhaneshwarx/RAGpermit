## Identity

NovaWorks is a fictional software company used for testing PermitRAG. It is based in Chennai and was founded in 2022. Its CEO is Dhanesh.

## Services

NovaWorks builds inventory software, customer support portals, and internal document search tools for small businesses. Its mission is to make everyday business information easier to use.

## Departments

Engineering builds and maintains software. HR manages hiring and employee support. Finance manages budgets and payments. Legal reviews contracts. Executive sets company strategy.
