## Marketing budget

The annual marketing budget is INR 20 lakh. Arjun reviews budget utilization on the fifth working day of each month.

## Expense approvals

Every expense above INR 50000 requires Finance Head approval. Expenses of INR 50000 or less require the reporting manager approval.

## Vendor payments

Standard vendor payment terms are 30 days after invoice approval. Invoices must include the purchase order number and delivery confirmation.
