## Review

Meera coordinates legal review. All vendor contracts require Legal Counsel review before signing.

## Termination

The standard customer contract requires 30 days written notice for termination. Exceptions must be approved by Legal Counsel.

## Confidentiality

A signed nondisclosure agreement is required before sharing confidential product plans with a vendor. Signed agreements are retained for seven years under this fictional policy.
