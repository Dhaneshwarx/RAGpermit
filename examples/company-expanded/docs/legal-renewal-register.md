## Renewal tracking

Fictional NovaWorks example. Meera maintains renewal dates and notice periods for active agreements. The internal reminder schedule is 60 days and 30 days before each renewal decision deadline.

## Business decision

Fictional NovaWorks example. The agreement owner confirms whether to renew, renegotiate or terminate. Legal checks the notice requirements before any termination notice is issued.

## Retention

Fictional NovaWorks example. Signed agreements are retained for seven years under the fictional company policy. Drafts and review comments are accessible only to authorized legal staff and approved reviewers.

## Nondisclosure agreements

Fictional NovaWorks example. A signed nondisclosure agreement is required before confidential product plans are shared with a vendor. Meera checks the parties, permitted purpose and confidentiality period.
