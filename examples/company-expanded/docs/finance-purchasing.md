## Purchase request

Fictional NovaWorks example. A purchase request includes item description, business purpose, quantity, estimated cost and department. Arjun checks budget availability before a purchase order is issued.

## Supplier comparison

Fictional NovaWorks example. Purchases above INR 100000 require three supplier quotations unless Arjun records an approved single-source justification. This is a fictional internal control.

## Invoice checks

Fictional NovaWorks example. Finance matches the invoice to the purchase order and delivery confirmation. Missing purchase order numbers or delivery evidence cause the invoice to be returned for correction.

## Payment schedule

Fictional NovaWorks example. Approved vendor invoices are paid according to the standard 30-day terms. Arjun reviews the payment queue every Thursday. Urgent exceptions require documented Finance Head approval.
