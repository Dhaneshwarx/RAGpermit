## Interview stages

Fictional NovaWorks example. Recruitment uses an HR screening, a role-specific assessment and a hiring-manager interview. Priya schedules all stages and records candidate consent for interview processing.

## Scoring

Fictional NovaWorks example. Interviewers score role knowledge, problem solving and communication from one to five. The hiring panel reviews candidates with an average score of at least four; a score alone does not constitute an offer.

## Feedback deadline

Fictional NovaWorks example. Interviewers submit written feedback within two working days. Feedback must describe job-related evidence and must not include unrelated personal assumptions.

## Offer approval

Fictional NovaWorks example. The department head approves the candidate recommendation. Arjun checks salary budget availability. Priya issues an offer only after the required approvals are recorded.
