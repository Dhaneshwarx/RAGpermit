## Delivery risk

Fictional NovaWorks example. Risk EX-01 is a delay in the NovaStock pilot caused by integration defects. Deepak owns mitigation through weekly integration tests and an issue review every Wednesday.

## Staffing risk

Fictional NovaWorks example. Risk EX-02 is delayed recruitment for Project Horizon. Priya owns mitigation through a weekly candidate review and maintaining two qualified backup candidates for each open role.

## Cash risk

Fictional NovaWorks example. Risk EX-03 is delayed payment from customers. Arjun reviews overdue invoices weekly and escalates invoices more than 45 days overdue to Dhanesh.

## Decision rule

Fictional NovaWorks example. Dhanesh approves project scope changes that affect quarterly objectives. A risk requiring additional funding must include the amount, business impact, alternatives and owner.
