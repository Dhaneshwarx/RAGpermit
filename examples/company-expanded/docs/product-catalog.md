## Inventory product

Fictional NovaWorks example. NovaStock is NovaWorks inventory software for small retailers. It tracks product quantities, purchase orders and low-stock alerts. The product owner is Deepak.

## Support product

Fictional NovaWorks example. NovaDesk is a customer support portal for service businesses. It offers ticket submission, ticket assignment and response tracking. It does not provide payroll or banking services.

## Document product

Fictional NovaWorks example. PermitRAG is an internal document question-answering project. It provides password login, permission-filtered search and source citations. Public internet hosting is not part of its current local setup.

## Customer onboarding

Fictional NovaWorks example. Customer onboarding has four steps: requirements meeting, sample-data preparation, guided product setup and acceptance review. A standard onboarding engagement targets ten working days after requirements are approved.
