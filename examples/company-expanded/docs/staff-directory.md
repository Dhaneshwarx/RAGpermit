## Team

Fictional NovaWorks example. Dhanesh is CEO in Executive. Priya is HR Manager in HR. Arjun is Finance Head in Finance. Deepak is Engineer in Engineering. Meera is Legal Counsel in Legal. Kavin is an Engineering intern.

## Responsibilities

Fictional NovaWorks example. Dhanesh approves strategic priorities. Priya handles hiring and employee support. Arjun handles budgets and invoice approvals. Deepak maintains backend services. Meera reviews contracts. Kavin completes supervised development tasks.

## Escalation

Fictional NovaWorks example. For leave questions contact Priya. For expense questions contact Arjun. For software access or outages contact Deepak. For contract questions contact Meera. Escalate unresolved cross-department priorities to Dhanesh.

## Communication

Fictional NovaWorks example. Use the internal company help desk for routine requests. Include the affected project, request description, urgency and desired completion date. Never include passwords or customer secrets in a request.
