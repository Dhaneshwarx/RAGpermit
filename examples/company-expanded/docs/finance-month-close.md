## Close schedule

Fictional NovaWorks example. The monthly close target is the fifth working day of the following month. Arjun owns the close checklist and signs off the completed management report.

## Reconciliation

Fictional NovaWorks example. Bank reconciliation is completed by the second working day. Receivable and payable reconciliations are completed by the third working day. Differences must have an assigned owner.

## Reporting

Fictional NovaWorks example. The monthly management report contains revenue, operating expenses, receivables aging and budget variance. A variance above ten percent requires a written explanation from the department owner.

## Collections

Fictional NovaWorks example. Finance contacts customers when invoices are seven days overdue. Invoices more than 45 days overdue are escalated to Dhanesh. No customer bank details are included in this sample document.
