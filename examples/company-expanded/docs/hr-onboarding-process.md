## Before joining

Fictional NovaWorks example. Priya verifies the signed offer, start date, department and reporting manager. She requests equipment and approved account access three working days before joining.

## First day

Fictional NovaWorks example. The first-day checklist includes identity verification, policy briefing, security training assignment and introduction to the reporting manager. Security training must finish within five working days.

## Probation

Fictional NovaWorks example. The fictional probation period is 90 days. The reporting manager provides reviews at day 30, day 60 and day 85. Priya records the final decision before day 90.

## Offboarding

Fictional NovaWorks example. Priya confirms the last working day with the manager. Engineering disables accounts by the end of that day. The departing employee returns equipment and transfers work before departure.
