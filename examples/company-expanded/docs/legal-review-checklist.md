## Intake

Fictional NovaWorks example. Meera requires the draft agreement, business owner, counterparty name, proposed start date and description of the service. Missing information is returned to the requester.

## Review areas

Fictional NovaWorks example. Legal review checks scope of work, payment terms, confidentiality, termination and responsibilities for handling data. This fictional checklist is a testing document, not legal advice.

## Turnaround

Fictional NovaWorks example. The internal target for an initial standard-contract review is five working days after complete intake. Unusual terms require a separate timeline agreed with the business owner.

## Approval record

Fictional NovaWorks example. Meera records the version reviewed and unresolved issues. The business owner confirms commercial details before an authorized company signatory signs the final agreement.
