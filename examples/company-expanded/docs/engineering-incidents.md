## Severity

Fictional NovaWorks example. SEV1 means the production service is unavailable to most users or there is suspected unauthorized data exposure. SEV2 means a major feature is unavailable but a workaround exists.

## Initial response

Fictional NovaWorks example. The on-call engineer acknowledges a SEV1 incident within 15 minutes and informs Deepak immediately. Deepak coordinates technical investigation and assigns an incident owner.

## Updates

Fictional NovaWorks example. The incident owner publishes an internal update every 30 minutes during a SEV1 incident. Updates contain impact, actions taken and the next update time, not passwords or secret keys.

## Follow-up

Fictional NovaWorks example. Within two working days after resolution, Deepak coordinates a review covering timeline, cause, customer impact and corrective actions. Every corrective action has an owner and target date.
