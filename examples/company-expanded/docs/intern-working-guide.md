## Daily routine

Fictional NovaWorks example. Kavin reviews assigned training issues at the start of each day and records progress before leaving. If blocked for more than one hour, he contacts Deepak.

## Weekly report

Fictional NovaWorks example. The weekly report is due Friday by 5 PM. It includes completed tasks, lessons learned, blockers and the next week plan. Send it through the approved internal reporting channel.

## Access boundaries

Fictional NovaWorks example. Interns use the training environment and approved sample data. Interns must not request production passwords from colleagues or copy customer data into training projects.

## Review requests

Fictional NovaWorks example. A review request includes the issue reference, a short description of the change and the checks performed. Kavin waits for mentor approval before marking a training issue complete.
